Here is a complete, single-file war strategy game built in HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. It is heavily inspired by the "dumb mapping" genre on YouTube and games like *War of Dots*. It includes all four map modes, custom map painting tools (terrain, borders, cities), an extensive country interaction and geopolitics engine (rebellion, alliances like NATO/BRICS, puppeteering), a dynamic frontline "string" system, and dual simulation/creative gameplay modes.
